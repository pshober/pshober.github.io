Dark flight

{% if df %}

After the fireball goes dark, meteorites fall freely through the wind. Each radar cell is flown down to the ground through the winds of that day; the coloured areas show where stones of each mass most likely landed, and the black line runs from light to heavy stones (solid where the radar traced it, dashed where extended). {% if df.auto and df.human %}The two maps use the same method and the same scale: one from MetDetect's cells, one from the cells identified by hand.{% if df.overlap %} Their 90% search areas for stones of 10 g and up overlap by {{ df.overlap | times: 100 | round }}% (shared area over combined area).{% endif %}{% endif %}
