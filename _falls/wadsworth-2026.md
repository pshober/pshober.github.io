NASA ARES lists this fall as Windfall, Ohio.

MetDetect's RUN-41 searched one radar, KCLE (Cleveland). KCLE saw only fine debris, about 0.1–0.6 g, late in
the fall, so the map from its cells lies north of the recovered stones. Other radars nearby, including the TLVE
airport radar (TDWR, Cleveland Hopkins), saw the heavier stones. Searching every radar that can
see a fall, airport radars included, is the next step for MetDetect.
