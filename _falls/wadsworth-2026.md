NASA ARES lists this fall as Windfall, Ohio.

MetDetect's RUN-42 searched one radar, KCLE (Cleveland), and found the fall: its object holds 240 of the 339
hand-identified cells there. The debris its cells trace weighs about 0.2–9 g, so the 10 g and up search area built from
them is an extrapolation along the fall line; it covers 6 of the 13 recovered stones of 10 g and up, and the others
lie further south. The hand identification also uses the KPBZ (Pittsburgh) echoes and the TLVE airport radar (TDWR, Cleveland Hopkins). MetDetect does not use airport radars yet; searching every radar that can see a fall, airport radars included, is the next step.
