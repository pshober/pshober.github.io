Ask about a fireball

Saw a bright fireball, or heard a boom, and wonder whether a weather radar caught falling meteorites? Send me the time and place. I can't promise to look at every request, but I read them all, and if the radar shows something I will reply.

  • A time range of up to an hour is fine. Use your local time; it is converted to UTC for you.
  • Give where you were, or where you think it came down. You can click the map.
  • Radar coverage is best in the USA; other countries are possible case by case.
